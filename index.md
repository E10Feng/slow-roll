  <h1>Slow Roll</h1>
  <p class="muted">Heads-up poker in iMessage.</p>

Play no-limit Texas Hold'em with one friend, right in Messages. Every move is a message, so a match can run for minutes or days, whenever each of you has a moment.

<div class="card" markdown="1">
- One clear screen: your cards, the board, the pot and both stacks
- A live label names your best hand as the board comes out
- Showdowns show who won, each hand's name and the deciding kicker
- $1,000 stacks, $5/$10 blinds, play until someone has all the chips
</div>

Chips are just for fun: no real money, no purchases, no ads, and no data collected.

Slow Roll is in testing and not on the App Store yet.

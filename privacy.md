# Privacy Policy

Slow Roll ("the app") is a two-player poker game that runs inside Apple's Messages app. Effective October 27, 2026.

## What we collect

Nothing. The developer runs no servers and has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party code in the app. The app never connects to the internet itself.

## How your games are sent

Each move is sent as an iMessage message between you and your opponent, through Apple's Messages service, like any other message you send. The message carries the game's state (cards, chips and moves) in encrypted form, plus an anonymous player identifier that Messages gives the app. That identifier is not your name, phone number, email or contacts, and the app only uses it to tell the two players apart. The developer never receives or sees these messages. Apple's own privacy policy covers how Messages handles them.

## What stays on your device

So matches stay in sync, the app saves recent game states and which hand results you've already seen, on your device only. Deleting the app deletes this data. Game messages already in a conversation stay there until you delete them in Messages.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and at https://e10feng.github.io/slow-roll/privacy/. For questions, see https://e10feng.github.io/slow-roll/support/.

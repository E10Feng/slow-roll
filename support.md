# Support

## Getting started

1. Open a conversation in **Messages** with the friend you want to play.
2. Tap **+** next to the text field, then **Slow Roll**. If you don't see it, tap **More** and scroll.
3. Tap **New match**. Make your move: it's sent as a message, and your friend taps it to reply.

Your friend needs Slow Roll installed too, on an iPhone or iPad with iMessage.

## Common questions

**I can't see my opponent's cards.** That's by design. Each player only ever sees their own two cards, until a showdown.

**How do I start over?** Tap **•••** on the table, then **Restart match**. Or tap **Resign** to concede.

**A message says "Update the app to keep playing."** Your friend has a newer version. Update Slow Roll from the App Store.

**A message says "This match couldn't be read."** Open the most recent move in the conversation instead.

**How do I see what happened earlier in the hand?** Tap **History** on the table.

Found a bug or have a question? [Open an issue](https://github.com/E10Feng/slow-roll/issues/new) and we'll get back to you.
